A LITTLE FAMILY COMPETITION

Get away. Stay out.

Play all your cards to escape. The last person holding cards loses.

  1. Ace of Spades opens. Everyone plays once; this first trick is always discarded.
  2. Follow the leading suit whenever you can. Aces are high.
  3. No matching suit? Play any card: Tulla! The trick stops immediately. The highest card in the leading suit picks up the pile and leads again.
  4. If everyone follows, discard the trick. The highest card leads again.
  5. If that leader has no cards, they draw from the previous waste and must lead it. Other empty hands escape.
  6. Before leading a normal trick, you may take the next active player’s whole hand, freeing them.

Final duel: a Tulla against a forced drawn lead ends the game. This edition follows Pagat’s Getaway rules; family variants differ.
